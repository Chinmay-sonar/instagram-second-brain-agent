# Text To Handwriting Converter Overview

Text to Handwriting is a free, web-based tool designed to convert digital text into realistic human handwriting. It is highly useful for students, educators, and professionals who need to generate handwritten assignments, notes, or personalized letters without writing them out physically.

### Key Capabilities & Features
- **Multiple Handwriting Styles:** Choose from 14 distinct human handwriting styles to find the one that best fits your needs.
- **Rich Text Formatting:** Supports bold, italic, underline, bulleted lists, numbered lists, and custom tables.
- **Mathematical Formula Support:** Easily insert simple mathematical formulas (e.g., `a^2`, `√x`, `1/2`) directly into the document.
- **Page Customization:**
  - **Ink Colors:** Blue, Black, and Red.
  - **Page Layouts:** Lined paper (notebook style) or Blank page (no lines).
  - **Page Size:** Standard A4 sizing.
  - **Spacing Adjustments:** Fine-tune font size, line spacing, and letter spacing to achieve a natural look.
- **Import Options:** Directly import existing PDF documents to convert their text content.
- **Export Formats:** Download generated pages as individual images or compile them into a single PDF document.

### Step-by-Step Guide to Use
1. **Input Text:** Type or paste your text into the main editor box.
2. **Style Selection:** Select one of the 14 handwriting styles from the dropdown menu.
3. **Formatting & Layout:** Choose your preferred ink color (Blue/Black/Red), page size, and paper style (lined or blank).
4. **Generate:** Click the **Generate Image** button to render the handwritten page preview.
5. **Download:** Export the final output as high-quality images or compile them into a downloadable PDF.
