### Research Internships Guide Breakdown

This guide provides a structured approach to securing research positions:

*   **Preparation:** Focuses on building a strong academic foundation, including GPA, relevant coursework, and research-oriented projects.

*   **Application Materials:** Best practices for creating a research-focused CV and writing compelling Statements of Purpose (SOP).
